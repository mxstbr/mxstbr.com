# My README

Hi, I’m Max 👋 If I sent you this, it's because we will work together and I want to set us up for success. The below is a user guide of sorts to explain what I care about, how I think, and some fun facts.

For a less polished, more ongoing view into what I’m thinking about, see my [/notes](/notes).

_[ENFP](https://www.16personalities.com/enfp-personality) MBTI · [Type 3](https://www.enneagraminstitute.com/type-3/) Enneagram_

## What I care about: shipping impactful things quickly with great people

> “Horizon is a major leap forward for our merchants, and it wouldn’t have happened without the intensity, focus, and execution you brought to the team. Big delivery.” – Harley Finkelstein, President of Shopify ([source](https://mxs.is/intense))

- **Impact:** Shipping impactful things…
  - Early in my career some of the open source projects I co-created (like [styled-components](https://github.com/styled-components/styled-components) and [react-boilerplate](https://github.com/react-boilerplate/react-boilerplate)) permanently influenced how the millions of software engineers in the React.js ecosystem build their apps.

  - That gave me a great sense of fulfillment, therefore shipping impactful things is now the bar for any work that I do.

- **Speed:** …quickly…
  - Good and fast aren't a tradeoff, you can have both. I love nothing more than proving this by shipping extremely ambitious things quickly.

  - Some examples: 1) how we shipped Horizon at Shopify ([mxs.is/intense](https://mxs.is/intense)) 2) [this inspiring collection of examples](https://patrickcollison.com/fast) by Patrick Collison

- **People:** …with great people.
  - In my experience, shipping ambitious things quickly requires a team of great people that are all going at it together. So those are the kinds of teams I strive to build.

## How I work

- **Win as one team**
  - I care a lot about belonging, connection, and trust because they are how ambitious teams actually move quickly.

  - I don't like hero mode. Share context, pull people in early, and make the people around you better.

- **Iterate quickly**
  - I love small loops: make a call, ship the smallest useful version, observe, learn, and go again.

  - Most decisions are reversible. If something is safe to try, I prefer doing it over discussing it for too long.

  - Progress over perfection.

- **Default to open**
  - Open work makes the team faster because people can make better decisions without waiting for meetings.

  - Put context, drafts, decisions, and learnings where others can see them unless there is a good reason not to.

- **Say the thing**
  - If something feels off, say it early, clearly, and kindly.

  - Make it about the work, not the person. Assume good intent, but don't let being nice get in the way of being clear.

  - I would much rather hear "I think this is wrong because..." than have the team silently drift toward a worse outcome.

- **My teams IRL**
  - We write things down, seek clarity, ask for feedback, and learn in the open.

  - We are kind, direct, curious, and willing to say no to good ideas so the most important work gets enough focus.

  - We make time for fun. Trust is a lot easier to build when people actually know each other.

## How I think

- As an ENFP, I’m an **intuition**-based decision-maker.
  - My brain creates a “graph” based on all the information I’ve collected that I can “feel out” to make decisions.

  - To retain specifics, I take copious notes in every meeting.

  - I will often repeat back what I understood from what you said to confirm our shared understanding.

- I **write to think**
  - Writing helps me bring focus to the graph in my brain. It’s also how I clarify and challenge my intuition.

- I always assume **good intent**
  - I believe people do things for good reasons, so I try hard to understand those reasons.

  - I ask that you extend the same courtesy to me.

- I **strive to be the best** at the things I care about
  - I am not a jack of all trades, and that’s intentional.

  - I am intentional about being various levels of world-class at a handful of things (e.g. table tennis, skiing, specialty coffee, Mario Kart, being a dad), and I love doing those things.

- I **love getting and giving feedback.**
  - I strive for rapid iteration of myself as a person. That’s why I value differing perspectives and seek feedback in everything I do.

  - The times of explosive growth in my life happened when I was in dynamic situations where I was reflected back to me. This enabled me to see the diff in who I thought I was, how I thought I was presenting, and how I was perceived externally.

- I’m happy to **change my mind**
  - Because the main thing I care about is making an impact, I have no problem changing my mind if a better reasoning or path comes up. I have low attachment to my thoughts, ideas, or opinions and will happily be convinced of a better decision.
